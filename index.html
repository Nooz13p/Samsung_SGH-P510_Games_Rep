About this preservation build

Evidence-informed playable approximations. P510 footage and the manual have not been accessed; this is not a verified replica.

BubbleSmile: select a triangle, then tap it again to rotate. Line up three of a color. Arrows or 2/4/6/8 move; Enter/5 rotates right; Space/1 rotates left. Reach the score target before time expires.

Fun2Link: tap to place the first queued pipe. Connect S to E through at least the indicated number of pipes before the water escapes. Arrows move; Enter/5 places; Space/* speeds water up. Wet pipes cannot be replaced.

Escape pauses. Enter resumes. Games returns to the launcher. Sound is an optional synthesized approximation. Progression and scoring are provisional.
